# zyflow-assets

Public brand assets (logos) for ZyflowSign — referenced by email templates.

## Lena.ai emails

`lena-email/` holds the logos every Lena.ai platform email shows: `lena-full.png` at the top of the card and `zyflow.png` in the "Powered by Zyflow" footer band. They are copies of the Lena.ai repo's `bridge/assets/email/` logos; update both together.
